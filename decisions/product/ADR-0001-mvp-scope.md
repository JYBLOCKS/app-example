# Product Decision: Approve the Smallest MVP Workflow

- Status: Proposed
- Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>

## Context

The product needs a narrow, testable launch scope. Broad role and workflow support can create unclear ownership and incomplete user journeys.

## Decision

Complete `specs/product/MVP_SCOPE.template.md` and approve only workflows with one primary actor, one explicit trigger, one happy path, and one observable success result. List every excluded workflow as a non-goal.

## Consequences

- Delivery is focused on complete workflows rather than partial modules.
- Deferred workflows require later product decisions.
