# Workflow: Make an Architecture Decision

1. Describe the problem without embedding a preferred solution.
2. List forces, constraints, and quality attributes.
3. Compare at least two viable alternatives when available.
4. Document the decision, consequences, and migration impact in an ADR.
5. Obtain the required human approval.
6. Update affected specifications, rules, registries, and templates.
7. Supersede conflicting older ADRs explicitly.
